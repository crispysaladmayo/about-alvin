01
AI Agent  ·  Production
RISA · Ringkas AI

Getting a home loan in Indonesia means calling multiple banks, submitting the same documents, and waiting weeks to find out you don't qualify. Banks spend sales time on leads that go nowhere. Ringkas aggregates across banks but was bottlenecked on manual qualification.

RISA is a WhatsApp agent that does the full intake: income, employment, liabilities, documents, eligibility. Qualifies the applicant and routes a warm lead to the right bank. LangChain backend, white-label config per bank, LLM-as-judge pipeline that vets every prompt version before it goes live. Built in the past 12 months.

Deployed as Ringkas's AI sales conversion agent across bank and developer clients. Converts inbound leads into qualified applications at scale, with no proportional headcount increase.

02
Platform Design
Ringkas Platform

Indonesian banks sell mortgages through human-led processes: manual data collection, document review, multi-round bank submissions. No standardized digital infrastructure for the full origination cycle existed.

Built the Ringkas LOS and Customer App from zero. The LOS connects banks on the backend via individual API integrations, handles the full application lifecycle (eligibility, document collection, bank submission, disbursement tracking), and enforces compliance rules per bank. The Customer App is the borrower-facing interface. Enterprise-grade: ISO 27001 certified, OJK compliant. Every bank integration passed security assessment in a single round.

2,000+ approved applications/month. 33 banks, 10 property developers. Most of the mortgage disbursement volume for our major clients now runs through this platform.
